📜 Hogwarts Wand Lore & Gestures

⚡

Expelliarmus

Gesture: Rapid defensive pullback followed by an aggressive forward thrust pointing at the opponent's wand.

🪶

Wingardium Leviosa

Gesture: "Swish and flick, remember, swish and flick!" Smooth upward arc with a crisp downward wrist snap.

💡

Lumos

Gesture: Upward flourish holding the wand like a lantern to ignite the holly core with celestial light.

🌙

Nox

Gesture: Sharp extinguishing cross-body swipe (like a candle snuffer) to extinguish the light.

🗝️

Alohomora

Gesture: Smooth clockwise spiral loop aimed at the keyhole to disengage enchanted brass tumblers.

📖

Accio

Gesture: Sharp circular lock-on with a backward summoning pull, preparing the free hand to catch the object.
